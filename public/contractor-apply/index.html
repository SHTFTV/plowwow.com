PlowWow handles winter work in-house and is building a reviewed list of contractors for equipment breakdowns, heavy events and additional demand beyond our own five-hour route window. We want to understand the support you can offer before a difficult storm arrives.

Current focus: Metro Vancouver and the Fraser Valley, including Langley, Abbotsford, Maple Ridge, Mission and Aldergrove. Contractors elsewhere can register interest for future expansion; this does not mean service is currently available in every submitted area.

From walkway crews to snow-hauling trucks

Apply for hand clearing, walkway treatment, brine application, granular salting, plowing, skid-steer or loader support, and snow relocation. We need to know the available equipment, operators, travel range and hours you can offer without compromising your existing clients.

How review works

  1. Submit your available capacity and coverage details below.
  2. PlowWow reviews the fit and may ask for current insurance certificates, equipment information and WorkSafe / WCB clarification or clearance.
  3. Any approved work is agreed directly: site, scope, dispatch, safety requirements, rates and payment terms.

An application does not put you on an active route or guarantee hours. No public contractor listing is created from your submission. Read why we are building backup capacity.
