# PlowWow Lower Mainland SEO / AEO / LLM Quality Gate

This file defines the publishing standard for PlowWow location and service content.

## Geographic scope

Primary rollout: Vancouver, Burnaby, Surrey, Richmond, Coquitlam, Port Coquitlam, Port Moody, New Westminster, North Vancouver, West Vancouver, Delta, White Rock, Langley, Maple Ridge, Pitt Meadows, Abbotsford, Mission and Chilliwack.

A city/service-area URL is not a claim that PlowWow maintains an office in that municipality.

## Page hierarchy

1. Homepage
2. Service hubs
3. `/locations`
4. City hubs
5. City x service pages only where the service is actually offered and the page has distinct useful content
6. Neighborhood/property-type pages only where there is enough specific information to justify an indexable page
7. Winter knowledge / municipal-information content that links back to relevant services and cities

## Every indexable local landing page must have

- unique title and meta description
- one clear H1
- self-referencing canonical
- breadcrumb navigation and BreadcrumbList JSON-LD
- accurate Organization/provider relationship
- Service schema where appropriate
- accurate areaServed without a fake local address
- useful city-specific introduction
- relevant property types and service applications
- local winter/terrain/access context that is supportable
- service process without invented guarantees
- FAQs written for actual customer questions
- related services
- nearby Lower Mainland city links
- clear call and quote actions
- no aggregateRating until real PlowWow review data exists
- no fake testimonials, awards, customer counts or local offices

## Municipal information

When a page discusses bylaws, sidewalk deadlines, snow-route rules or property-owner obligations, the statement must be based on a current authoritative municipal source. Do not copy one city's rules onto another city page. Content should distinguish municipal requirements from PlowWow's own service terms.

## LLM / AEO standard

Write explicit answers that can stand alone when extracted by a search engine or AI system. Define the service, who it is for, what property information is needed, what affects timing and pricing, and which geographic area the page covers. Avoid keyword-stuffed variants and unsupported superlatives.

## Review/reputation policy

PlowWow is being established as its own brand/reputation entity. Until real reviews are available, do not publish stars, review counts, aggregateRating schema, fabricated testimonials, `top rated`, or equivalent social-proof claims. Add verified reputation only when the source is current and attributable to PlowWow.

## Operational claim policy

Claims such as 24/7 response, GPS-tracked fleet, monitoring, guaranteed response windows, automatic dispatch, insurance limits, specific equipment availability and fixed completion times must only be published if currently true for PlowWow and applicable to the scope described. Otherwise use accurate language such as `subject to availability`, `confirmed in the service agreement`, or remove the claim.
