## Planning better handovers between crews, councils and residents

Snow Intelligence is in its first year of beta. Our founder reports 15 seasons in snow removal, four seasons marketing across North America through a previous brand, and 25 years in contracting. That experience informs the questions; the new software still has to prove its usefulness through testing.

We believe the combination of practical operating knowledge, AI and business ingenuity can improve winter-service communication. We are proud of the direction. We do not claim independent best-in-market status or expertise over a technology field that is changing this quickly.

## What can I try today?

Our [public pricing beta](/snow-removal-calculator) provides a provisional starting point for an enquiry. A contractor still reviews the property, scope, written terms and route capacity. A displayed estimate does not book a crew or confirm app access.

Ask PlowWow which client views, notifications, photos and visit records are enabled and tested for your property. Property-specific availability must be confirmed directly.

## A couple of ideas shaping the beta

**Pre-season hazard mapping:** record visible curbs, hydrants, drains and snow-stacking areas before they are covered. An augmented-reality overlay is a development concept, not a validated whiteout-navigation system.

**Clearer service records:** help crews and property managers distinguish arrival, work recorded, an unresolved exception and a later condition report. These are workflow goals, not a claim that every property already receives automated updates.

## Advanced capabilities need evidence

AI photo review, salt-density measurement, weather directives, seven-year vault retention, accounting connections, contract-triggered billing and maintained bylaw references remain under evaluation unless availability is specifically confirmed. We do not advertise a photograph as a calibrated grams-per-square-metre measurement, an AI compliance certificate or an automatic approval to release or withhold payment.

Records can support a service review; they do not make a site liability-proof, guarantee court acceptance or promise lower insurance premiums. Material rates require competent site assessment, suitable equipment and applicable product instructions.

## Help us make it useful in your region

Metro Vancouver and Fraser Valley properties raise different practical questions: tight ramps and curbs, larger site boundaries, access restrictions, treatment records and route planning. Tell us which two problems would make the greatest difference at your property. We would rather test a useful workflow carefully than publish a long unverified feature list.
