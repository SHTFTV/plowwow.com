### Richmond Winter Climate

Richmond has one of the mildest winter climates in Metro Vancouver due to its low elevation and proximity to the Pacific Ocean. Snowfall is infrequent and typically occurs during short Arctic outflow events between December and February. Accumulation is usually light and melts quickly, particularly in coastal and low-lying areas near the Fraser River delta.

## Historical Snowfall Data

Average monthly snowfall: **January** ~85 mm, **February** ~65 mm, **December** ~90 mm, **March** ~25 mm.
