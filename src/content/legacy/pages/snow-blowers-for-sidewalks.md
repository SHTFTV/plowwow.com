# Snow Blowers For Sidewalks

PlowWow operates a fleet of commercial-grade **walk-behind snow blowers** to keep sidewalks, walkways, building entries and pedestrian access safe across Greater Vancouver.

## Where We Use Them

- Strata and condo walkways
- Apartment building entries and stairs
- Commercial storefronts and customer access
- Strip mall sidewalks
- Long residential walks and side paths

## Why It Matters

Sidewalks are the highest slip-and-fall risk on any property. Plows can't reach them — only proper sidewalk crews with snow blowers, shovels and salt can. PlowWow includes sidewalk service in every seasonal contract.

## What You Get

- Walk-behind snow blowers for fast, complete clearing
- Hand shoveling on stairs and tight areas
- Salt and ice melt application
- Photo-documented service every visit
- 24/7 automatic dispatch
