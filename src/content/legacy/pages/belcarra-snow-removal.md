### Belcarra Winter Climate

Belcarra sits between forested mountain slopes and Burrard Inlet, creating a **unique winter micro-climate**. Snowfall is significantly heavier than Port Moody and Vancouver, especially in inland and wooded regions. Snow can begin as early as **mid-November** and may remain on rural roads until **April**, especially near Bedwell Bay Road, Tum-Tumay-Whueton Drive, and the entrance to Belcarra Regional Park.

## Historical Snowfall Data

Monthly averages: **January** ~185 mm, **February** ~165 mm, **December** ~210 mm, **March** ~90 mm.
