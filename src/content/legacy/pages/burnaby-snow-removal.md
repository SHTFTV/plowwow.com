### Burnaby Winter Climate

Burnaby experiences a coastal winter climate with slightly higher snowfall than downtown Vancouver due to increased elevation in several neighborhoods. Snowfall events typically occur between late November and early March, with Burnaby Mountain and higher inland areas receiving more frequent and longer- lasting accumulation compared to low-lying regions.

## Historical Snowfall Data

Average monthly snowfall: **January** ~120 mm, **February** ~105 mm, **December** ~135 mm, **March** ~45 mm.
