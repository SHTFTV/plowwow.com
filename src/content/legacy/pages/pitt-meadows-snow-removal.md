### H2: Understanding Pitt Meadows’ Unique Winter Weather

Pitt Meadows experiences distinctive winter conditions due to its unique geography—surrounded by the Pitt River, Fraser River, and Pitt-Addington Marsh, with the Coast Mountains visible to the north. This creates specific winter challenges that require experienced local snow removal expertise.

**River and Marsh Effects:** Pitt Meadows’ proximity to extensive waterways and marshlands creates unique moisture conditions. Freezing fog is common, creating frost and ice even without precipitation. Morning frost can persist longer than in surrounding communities, especially near water and low-lying areas.

**Valley Snow Events:** Situated in the Fraser Valley, Pitt Meadows receives Pacific moisture systems that produce moderate to heavy snowfall when cold air meets valley moisture. Snow accumulation can be significant during these events.

**Flat Terrain Advantages:** Unlike nearby hillside communities, Pitt Meadows’ relatively flat terrain means fewer slope-related challenges but more persistent cold air pooling in low areas, creating stubborn ice conditions.

**Dyke System Considerations:** Properties along the extensive dyke system can experience different conditions with exposure to wind and moisture from the rivers, affecting snow accumulation and ice formation patterns.

**Agricultural Land Microclimates:** Open agricultural lands can experience different conditions than residential areas—wind-driven snow redistribution, cold air settling, and unique frost patterns that affect farm operations.

**Airport Proximity:** Areas near Pitt Meadows Regional Airport may experience slightly different conditions due to open terrain and runway weather monitoring.

**Freeze-Thaw Cycles:** Pitt Meadows’ valley position and moisture influence create frequent freeze-thaw cycles. Daytime melting followed by overnight freezing produces hazardous ice, especially on north-facing surfaces and shaded areas near vegetation and structures.

These unique challenges require a snow removal team that truly understands Pitt Meadows’ geography, community character, and seasonal patterns—that’s where PlowWow’s local expertise makes all the difference.
