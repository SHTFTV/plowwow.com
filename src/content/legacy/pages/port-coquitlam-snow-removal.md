### Port Coquitlam Winter Climate

Port Coquitlam experiences a mild coastal winter climate with periodic snowfall events. Snow typically falls between late November and early March, with January and February being the most active months. Due to its location near the Pitt River and Coquitlam River, snow accumulation varies by neighborhood and elevation, often melting quickly in lower-lying areas.

## Historical Snowfall Data

Average monthly snowfall: **January** ~110 mm, **February** ~95 mm, **December** ~80 mm, **March** ~35 mm.
