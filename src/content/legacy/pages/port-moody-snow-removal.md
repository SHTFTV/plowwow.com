## Winter Weather Overview

### Port Moody Winter Climate

Port Moody experiences a **cooler and wetter inland winter climate** compared to coastal cities. Its proximity to **mountains, Burrard Inlet, and forested elevation zones** results in more snowfall than Vancouver and Richmond. Snow commonly falls between late November and early March, with longest-lasting accumulation in **Heritage Mountain and Anmore-adjacent neighborhoods**.

Average monthly snowfall: **January** ~140 mm, **February** ~120 mm, **December** ~160 mm, **March** ~55 mm.
