### Vancouver Winter Climate

Vancouver has a mild coastal winter climate influenced by the Pacific Ocean. Snowfall is relatively infrequent and typically occurs during Arctic outflow events between December and February. When snow does occur, accumulation can disrupt transportation due to limited snow persistence at sea level. Snowfall varies by neighborhood, with higher elevations such as Burnaby Mountain edges and southern Vancouver receiving slightly more accumulation.

## Historical Snowfall Data

Average monthly snowfall: **January** ~110 mm, **February** ~90 mm, **December** ~120 mm, **March** ~40 mm.
