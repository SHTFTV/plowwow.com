## Winter Weather Overview

### Anmore Winter Climate

Anmore is a **mountain-rimmed rural community above Port Moody**, where elevation and forested landscape contribute to **much colder temperatures and more persistent snow** than surrounding Tri-Cities municipalities. Snow commonly begins in **November** and can remain on roads through **early April**, especially near **Heritage Woods, Sunnyside Road, and Buntzen Lake**.

## Historical Snowfall Data

Monthly averages: **January** ~215 mm, **February** ~185 mm, **December** ~240 mm, **March** ~120 mm.
