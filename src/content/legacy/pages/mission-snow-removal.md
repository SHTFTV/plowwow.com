Mission experiences moderate winter snowfall, with snow falling primarily between November and April. ### H2: Understanding Mission’s Unique Winter Weather

Mission experiences distinctive winter conditions due to its location where the Fraser River valley narrows and the Coast Mountains rise sharply to the north. This unique geography creates specific winter challenges that require experienced local snow removal expertise.

**Fraser Valley Snow Events:** Mission sits in the heart of the Fraser Valley snow belt, often receiving significant snowfall when Pacific moisture meets cold air. Mission can receive more snow than downriver communities, with accumulations varying dramatically between riverside and mountain-adjacent areas.

**Elevation Variations:** Properties in Silverdale and other hillside neighborhoods experience significantly different conditions than valley floor properties. Higher elevations see more snow accumulation, longer-lasting cold temperatures, and more persistent ice conditions. Morning frost and ice linger longer on north-facing hillside properties.

**Arctic Outflows:** Cold air masses from the Interior pour down the Fraser Canyon, and Mission often feels these effects intensely. These events bring sudden temperature drops, strong winds, and create dangerous black ice conditions, particularly on the Mission Bridge, hillside roads, and shaded valley areas.

**Microclimate Variations:** Mission’s diverse terrain creates numerous microclimates. Riverside properties near the Fraser experience milder conditions but more fog and frost. Mountain-adjacent properties face colder temperatures and heavier snow. Rural areas in Hatzic and Dewdney can experience conditions different from downtown.

**Freeze-Thaw Cycles:** Mission’s position in the valley means frequent temperature fluctuations that create challenging freeze-thaw cycles. Daytime melting followed by overnight freezing produces hazardous ice, especially on driveways, walkways, and north-facing surfaces.

**Terrain Challenges:** Mission’s topography includes steep hillsides, long rural driveways, elevation changes, and varied property types that require different snow removal approaches and specialized equipment.

These unique challenges require a snow removal team that truly understands Mission’s geography, diverse neighborhoods, and seasonal patterns—that’s where PlowWow’s local expertise and experience make all the difference.
