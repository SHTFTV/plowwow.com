## Frequently Asked Questions

### What weather data does PlowWow use to dispatch crews?
PlowWow combines live forecast feeds, hyper-local snow gauges across the Lower Mainland and on-the-ground reporting from operators to trigger dispatch — typically when accumulation reaches 2–3 inches at a site.

### Do I need to call to request service?
No. Contracted properties are dispatched automatically when trigger depth is reached. Calls are only required to add ad-hoc visits or change service scope.

### Is every truck GPS tracked?
Yes. Every PlowWow vehicle reports location and timestamp data continuously, which feeds into route optimization and per-property service logs.

### How does PlowWow handle freezing rain or ice events?
Ice events are tracked separately from snowfall. Pre-storm salting is dispatched ahead of the event when forecasts show freezing rain, sleet or temperature swings around 0°C.

### Can I see service records for my property?
Yes. Photo and GPS service logs are available to property managers and strata councils on request, and can be delivered as monthly summaries.
