### 🌨️ **Local Weather & Snow Patterns**

While snow in Burnaby isn’t as heavy as in interior BC, the city does experience **seasonal winter weather** that affects properties differently depending on elevation and microclimate:

*   **Metrotown and South Burnaby** (Lower elevation) often see milder conditions but frequent freeze–thaw cycles that create ice hazards.

*   **Burnaby North & Burnaby Highlands** can receive **heavier snowfall accumulations**, especially during cold Pacific storms.

*   **Burnaby Lake and Central Burnaby** areas often turn slushy before refreezing, making snow and ice removal tricky.

Burnaby’s winter weather includes:

*   Frequent rain that transitions to snow at cooler temperatures

*   Freeze–thaw cycles that form ice patches

*   Occasional snow events that require immediate snow removal

This variation means that **strategic snow removal and ice management** are essential for keeping properties safe and accessible — and that’s where PlowWow operators shine.

### 📜 **Historical & Local Snow Notes**

Burnaby’s winter weather history reflects variability:

*   Years with **light snow totals**, and other years with **significant accumulations** — especially at higher elevations.

*   Freeze–thaw patterns can make ice more dangerous than snow itself.

*   Lower‑lying areas near bodies of water like **Burnaby Lake** often deal with **icy sidewalks and parking lots** even when heavier snow has melted.

Local landmarks like **Central Park**, **Deer Lake**, and **Burnaby Mountain** are useful markers for microclimate differences — and PlowWow operators take these into account when planning efficient snow removal routes and priorities.
