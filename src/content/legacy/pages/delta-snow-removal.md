### Delta Winter Climate

Delta experiences a **coastal winter climate** with generally mild temperatures, especially in **Tsawwassen and Ladner** where ocean influence reduces snowfall. **North Delta**, situated at a slightly higher elevation and farther inland, receives **significantly more snow** and colder nighttime temperatures.

## Historical Snowfall Data

Average monthly snowfall: **January** ~75 mm, **February** ~55 mm, **December** ~90 mm, **March** ~30 mm.

How much does snow removal cost in Delta?

Do you provide 24/7 snow removal service in Delta?

Yes. PlowWow operates 24/7 across Delta, monitoring weather conditions and automatically dispatching crews when snowfall reaches trigger depth. This ensures clearing even during nighttime storms, holidays, and weekends.

What areas of Delta do you service?

We service all areas of Delta, including North Delta, Ladner, Tsawwassen, Boundary Bay, Beach Grove, Sunbury, Nordel, Scottsdale, Annieville, and all residential, commercial, industrial, and farm properties across Delta.

Do I need to call you when it snows?

No. With automated service, snowfall is monitored 24/7 and our team is dispatched once accumulation meets your contracted trigger depth (typically 2–3 inches). No call is required—service is proactive.

What’s included in your seasonal snow removal contract?

Seasonal contracts include unlimited plowing, walkway and sidewalk clearing, salting, de-icing, and return visits when melting snow refreezes. Seasonal pricing helps homeowners, commercial tenants, and strata councils avoid unexpected bills.

Do you provide salting and de-icing in Delta?

Yes. Delta often experiences freeze–thaw caused by coastal air and humidity. We apply salt, brine, and de-icer proactively to prevent slips, focusing especially on shaded areas, walkways, steps, parking zones, and business entrances.

When should I sign up for snow removal in Delta?

The best time to register is September or October. Seasonal service capacity is limited, and early sign-up guarantees a spot, locks in pricing, and ensures priority response during storms.

Do you service strata complexes and commercial properties?

Yes. We handle townhouse complexes, condo buildings, office parks, industrial sites, farm access roads, retail plazas, and parking lots. Detailed reports and timestamps are available for strata managers and property owners.

Do you service farms and rural areas in Delta?

Yes. Delta has extensive agricultural zones and acreage lots. We are equipped to clear long driveways, barn entrances, farm-to-road connections, and private roads. Early-morning priority clearing is available for farm operations.

How do you handle Delta’s freeze–thaw and coastal snow patterns?

Delta frequently experiences wet snow that turns to ice. We pre-treat areas when storms are forecast, return for post-melting re-freezes, and focus on low-lying frost-prone areas such as Boundary Bay and Tsawwassen waterfront.
