# Residential Snow Removal

Snow clearing and de-icing planned around your home, driveway, walks, steps and entrance areas.

## Booking options

Seasonal plans include unlimited visits for the agreed scope. Five-visit residential bookings start at $125 per snow-clearing visit; on-call visits start at $150 subject to availability. Starting rates cover a straightforward sidewalk or walkway-only stop. Driveways, stairs, inclines and access conditions affect the quote.

For per-visit bookings, salting-only visits cost 50% of the applicable snow-clearing visit rate plus the de-icer material used.

## Seasonal terms

A five-hour planned return window applies in normal conditions; the agreement defines its start and scope. Access problems, prolonged snowfall and equipment disruption can affect timing. There is no seasonal visit cap.

An extreme-event surcharge of 40% of the agreed per-event baseline applies to continuous snowfall of 12 hours or more, or an event of 20 cm or more. The agreement defines the baseline and event measurement.
