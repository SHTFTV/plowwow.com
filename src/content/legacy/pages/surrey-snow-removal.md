### Surrey Winter Climate

Surrey experiences a mild coastal winter climate with periodic snowfall events. Snow typically falls between late November and early March, with the most frequent accumulation occurring in January and February. Snowfall varies across the city, with inland and northern neighborhoods generally receiving more snow than areas closer to the Fraser River and Boundary Bay.

## Historical Snowfall Data

Average monthly snowfall: **January** ~115 mm, **February** ~95 mm, **December** ~100 mm, **March** ~35 mm.
