## Winter Weather Overview

### Annual Snowfall

375 mm (14.76 inches)

### Snow Days Per Year

Approximately 20 days

### Peak Snow Months

December - February

### Chilliwack Winter Climate

Chilliwack experiences moderate winter snowfall, with snow falling primarily between November and April. The city receives an average of about 20 days of snowfall annually, with December and January being the snowiest months. Winter temperatures typically range from -3°C to 1°C during the day. The region's location in the Fraser Valley means snow accumulation can vary depending on elevation and specific location.

## Historical Snowfall Data

### Monthly Snowfall Averages

**December:** ~70 mm (2.76 inches) | **January:** ~103 mm (4.06 inches) | **February:** ~103 mm (4.06 inches) | **March & April:** Varying, generally decreasing
