# Snow Relocation & Hauling

When snow piles take over visitor stalls, sightlines or sidewalks, PlowWow's heavy equipment moves it out of the way — or off the property entirely.

## Our Snow Relocation Services

- **On-site relocation** — repositioning piles to safe, approved areas
- **Snow hauling** — loading and trucking snow off the property
- **Skid-steer & loader work** for heavy multi-event accumulation
- **Site planning** — designated piling zones built into your seasonal contract
- **Proper disposal** at approved snow dumps

## Why It Matters

By mid-winter, on-site piles can block sightlines, fire access, drainage and visitor parking. Snow relocation is what keeps strata, commercial and apartment sites functional through the back half of the season.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### When is snow relocation needed?
Snow relocation is typically needed by mid-winter when on-site piles start blocking visitor stalls, sightlines, fire access or drainage. Multi-event accumulation on strata, apartment and commercial sites is the most common trigger.

### Does PlowWow haul snow off the property?
Yes. For sites without room for further on-site piling, PlowWow loads and trucks snow to approved snow-disposal locations.

### Can snow relocation be added mid-season?
Yes. Existing contracted properties can add relocation visits at any point during the season — most sites add it once piles approach a metre in height.

### What equipment is used?
PlowWow uses skid-steers, loaders and dump trucks for relocation and hauling, sized to the site's access constraints.

### Where does the hauled snow go?
Hauled snow is taken to municipally approved snow-disposal sites. PlowWow does not dump snow onto streets, storm drains or unapproved private land.
