# Snow Relocation & Hauling

When snow piles take over visitor stalls, sightlines or sidewalks, PlowWow's heavy equipment moves it out of the way — or off the property entirely.

## Our Snow Relocation Services

- **On-site relocation** — repositioning piles to safe, approved areas
- **Snow hauling** — loading and trucking snow off the property
- **Skid-steer & loader work** for heavy multi-event accumulation
- **Site planning** — designated piling zones built into your seasonal contract
- **Proper disposal** at approved snow dumps

## Why It Matters

By mid-winter, on-site piles can block sightlines, fire access, drainage and visitor parking. Snow relocation is what keeps strata, commercial and apartment sites functional through the back half of the season.
