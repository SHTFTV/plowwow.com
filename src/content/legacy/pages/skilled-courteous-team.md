# Skilled & Courteous Snow Removal Team

PlowWow's crews are trained, equipped and accountable. Every operator on a PlowWow truck has been through our internal winter-operations program — from plow control and salt rates to property care and customer communication.

## What "Skilled & Courteous" Means at PlowWow

- **Trained operators** — every driver runs through plow, blower and de-icing certification before their first shift
- **Property-first mindset** — crews work to protect lawns, curbs, gates and landscaping
- **Clear communication** — text and email updates before, during and after major events
- **Uniformed and identifiable** — easy to recognize on your property
- **Local teams** — we live and work in the same communities we serve

## Why It Matters

Snow removal is high-pressure, high-liability work. The wrong operator damages property, misses sidewalks or shows up late. PlowWow's hiring, training and accountability process is built so the people on your site are the people you'd want there.
