### Abbotsford Winter Climate

Abbotsford experiences moderate winter conditions with snow falling primarily between November and April. The city receives an average of about 18 days of snowfall annually, with December through February being the snowiest months. Winter temperatures typically range from -2°C to 2°C during the day. As BC's fifth largest city located in the heart of the Fraser Valley, Abbotsford's snow accumulation can vary by neighborhood and elevation, with areas like Sumas Mountain seeing more snow than lower-lying regions.
