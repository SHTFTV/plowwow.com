White Rock Snow & Winter Weather Information

## Winter Weather Overview

### White Rock Winter Climate

White Rock has one of the **mildest winter climates** in the Lower Mainland, heavily influenced by its **coastal location on Semiahmoo Bay**. Snowfall is rare and usually melts quickly, typically occurring only during Arctic outflow events. Inland neighborhoods closer to King George Blvd may see slightly more accumulation.

## Historical Snowfall Data

Average monthly snowfall: **January** ~65 mm, **February** ~45 mm, **December** ~80 mm, **March** ~20 mm.
