## Frequently Asked Questions

### What does PlowWow's satisfaction guarantee actually cover?
If a PlowWow visit isn't to standard, we return and re-clear the affected area at no additional charge for the same snow event. This applies to plowing, hand shoveling, salting and de-icing.

### How quickly will PlowWow respond to a re-clear request?
Same-event re-clears are dispatched on priority — typically within hours during an active storm, depending on conditions and route load.

### Is there a limit on re-clears per storm?
No. Contracted properties get unlimited re-clears for the same event when conditions warrant additional service.

### Does the guarantee apply to seasonal contracts only?
The full re-clear guarantee applies to seasonal contracts. Per-visit / on-call work is covered by our standard quality commitment but is billed per visit.

### How do I report an issue?
Call your account lead or email Wow@PlowWow.com with your address. Photo evidence is helpful but not required.
