### Coquitlam Winter Climate

Coquitlam has a temperate Pacific Maritime climate with generally mild, wet winters and occasional snowfall. Snow tends to fall mainly from late November through March, with January and February typically being the snowiest periods. Snow amounts vary year-to-year and accumulations do not always remain on the ground for long in lowland areas. Coquitlam receives significant precipitation overall, and a portion of winter precipitation falls as snow rather than rain. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}
