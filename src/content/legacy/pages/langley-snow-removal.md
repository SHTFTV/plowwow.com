### Langley Winter Climate

Langley experiences a cooler inland winter climate compared to coastal Metro Vancouver. Snowfall is more frequent and tends to linger longer, particularly in Langley Township where elevation and open farmland contribute to colder overnight temperatures. Snow typically occurs between late November and early March, with January and February being the most active months.

## Historical Snowfall Data

Average monthly snowfall: **January** ~130 mm, **February** ~110 mm, **December** ~140 mm, **March** ~50 mm.
