## Westside Landlord Liability — Occupiers Liability Act and City Bylaw Duty

Arbutus Ridge property owners and strata corporations carry two overlapping duties every Vancouver winter.

Under the *Occupiers Liability Act* (BC), an occupier owes a duty to take that care that in all the circumstances of the case is reasonable to see that a person on the premises will be reasonably safe. In westside strata case law, "reasonable" has meant a written, pre-9 a.m. clearing protocol on every walkway a resident, visitor or tenant uses.

Under City of Vancouver Street and Traffic By-law No. 2849, property owners are required to clear ice and snow from public sidewalks adjacent to their property by 10 a.m. the day after a snowfall. Missed sidewalks on the West Boulevard, West 16th and Arbutus Street commercial frontages generate ticketing complaints faster than any other westside corridor.

What this means for an Arbutus Ridge strata council or landlord:

- **The 10 a.m. bylaw window is a floor, not a target.** Estate and retail sites need to be clear by first-light foot traffic — 7 a.m. on commercial, 8 a.m. on residential.
- **Photo documentation isn't optional.** Slip-and-fall plaintiffs subpoena the service log. If it doesn't exist, the defence starts on the back foot.
- **Heritage surfaces need explicit scope.** Any contract that says "walkways" without naming flagstone, brick, tile and stair landings is under-scoped for Arbutus Ridge.

## Strata Council Checklist for Arbutus Ridge

For Arbutus Ridge stratas, sections 3 and 72 of the *Strata Property Act* place responsibility for common-property safety squarely on the corporation. That includes every entrance walkway, mail kiosk, guest parking apron, ramp entrance, stair landing and shared mews.

Before signing any Arbutus Ridge strata snow contract, verify:

- **WorkSafeBC clearance letter** — current, not expired.
- **$5,000,000 commercial general liability** with the strata named as additional insured.
- **Commercial auto liability** on every plow and salt unit.
- **Written trigger threshold** in centimetres, and a written response-time commitment from trigger.
- **De-icer product disclosure** — magnesium chloride, calcium chloride, calcium magnesium acetate, or rock salt? Get it in writing. Heritage brick and concrete need CMA or magnesium chloride, never rock salt.
- **Subcontractor disclosure** — direct employees or subs? What are the subs' credentials?
- **Documentation deliverables** — GPS breadcrumbs, timestamped photos, weekly summaries, end-of-season AGM package.

If any one of those is missing, the contract is not defensible on a live claim.
