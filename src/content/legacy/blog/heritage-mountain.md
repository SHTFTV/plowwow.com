#### Essential Winter Safety Tips

## Stay Prepared This Winter on Burke Mountain

Ensure your safety and preparedness this winter with these essential tips: equip your vehicle with winter tires, keep emergency supplies handy, and clear snow from your vehicle before driving. Remember to salt walkways before freezing temperatures and report any hazardous conditions to your strata council. Stay informed by signing up for mountain weather alerts and always plan alternate routes for emergencies. Check on neighbors during heavy snowfalls and ensure your parking allows for snow removal equipment access.
