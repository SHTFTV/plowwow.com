## 🚍 Transit, Bus Schedules & Road Conditions

Snow and ice can impact transit reliability. TransLink prioritizes bus routes and implements anti-icing measures, but delays and service changes may occur. Check your route before leaving and allow extra travel time. ([TransLink Winter Conditions](https://www.translink.ca/winterconditions))

The City of Vancouver maintains major roads and emergency routes, but residential streets may be cleared later. Property owners are required to clear snow and ice from adjacent sidewalks within 10 hours after snowfall ends to comply with local bylaws. ([Vancouver Snow & Road Info](https://vancouver.ca/streets-transportation/snow-removal-from-city-streets.aspx))

## 🏫 School Closures & Winter Safety

Schools in Vancouver aim to remain open whenever possible, but closures or delayed openings are issued if conditions are unsafe. Updates are typically posted early in the morning via school district websites and social channels.
