## Local bylaws and liability — what Steveston property owners must know

Clearance of snow and ice in Steveston is governed by the **City of Richmond Traffic Bylaw No. 5870**. This bylaw dictates that all owners or occupiers of commercial and multi-family residential properties must remove snow and ice from sidewalks adjacent to their property no later than 10:00 AM every day, including Sundays and holidays.

Failure to comply can result in fines, but the greater risk is civil liability. In Steveston's humid environment, "black ice" is a frequent culprit for slip-and-fall accidents. If a pedestrian is injured on your unshovelled sidewalk, you could be held liable for damages. PlowWow's automated scheduling ensures that your property is cleared well before the 10:00 AM deadline, fulfilling your legal obligations and protecting your assets.

Black ice is common in Steveston because of the high humidity and the tendency for temperatures to hover just above freezing during the day and drop just below freezing at night.
