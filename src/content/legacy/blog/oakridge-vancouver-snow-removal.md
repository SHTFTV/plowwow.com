## Salt, De-Icer & the Environment

PlowWow uses a tiered de-icing approach in Oakridge:

- **Standard rock salt** (sodium chloride) for parking lots and fire lanes where there is no landscape contact and temperatures are above -10 °C.
- **Magnesium chloride brine** for pre-treatment before storms — it bonds to pavement and keeps the first inch of snow from sticking, which dramatically reduces the total salt load over the season.
- **Calcium chloride blends** for very cold events (below -10 °C) where standard salt stops working.
- **Pet-safe / landscape-safe de-icer** for strata walkways, residential driveways, and any frontage with mature landscaping or pet traffic — the Oakridge greenway is heavily dog-walked.

We log salt application by weight per visit so your property has a defensible record for liability and for any environmental compliance reporting.
