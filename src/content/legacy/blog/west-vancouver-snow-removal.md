## Why West Vancouver needs specialized snow & ice management

West Vancouver requires specialized snow management because of its extreme elevation changes, high-value landscaping, and localized weather patterns known as orographic lift. Unlike the flatter terrain of Richmond or Vancouver, West Vancouver properties often sit on steep inclines that turn treacherous with just a few millimetres of ice, requiring high-traction machinery and precision application of de-icers.

### The Cypress Mountain Effect
The proximity to Cypress Mountain creates a unique meteorological phenomenon. As moisture-rich Pacific air hits the North Shore Mountains, it rises and cools rapidly. This often results in heavy snowfall at higher elevations (above 200 metres) while Ambleside might only see rain. This "line of snow" moves throughout the day, requiring a snow removal partner that monitors elevation-specific weather data rather than general regional forecasts.

### Protecting Environmentally Sensitive Watersheds
Many West Vancouver neighbourhoods, such as those bordering Capilano River or Rodgers Creek, are situated near sensitive watersheds. Standard rock salt can damage delicate local ecosystems and leach into the soil, harming the mature cedars and manicured gardens that define West Coast modern estates. PlowWow utilizes eco-conscious, low-chloride de-icers and liquid brines that provide superior melting power without the environmental footprint of traditional methods.

## Local bylaws and liability — what West Vancouver property owners must know

In the District of West Vancouver, property owners are legally responsible for clearing snow and ice from sidewalks adjacent to their property. According to Traffic and Parking Bylaw No. 4370, residents and business owners must remove snow from sidewalks by 10:00 a.m. every day, including Sundays and holidays.

Failure to comply can result in municipal fines, but more importantly, it opens the property owner to significant civil liability if a pedestrian slips and falls. For strata corporations and commercial managers, the Duty of Care is even higher. PlowWow’s professional services are designed to meet and exceed these municipal requirements, providing a documented trail of maintenance that protects you from potential litigation.

Furthermore, West Vancouver has strict noise bylaws. Our fleet utilizes modern, well-maintained equipment designed to operate as quietly as possible, ensuring we clear your property without disturbing the tranquility of your neighbourhood or the sleep of your family.
