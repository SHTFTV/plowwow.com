## Downtown PoCo: follow the route from parking to the door

Wilson Medical is in **Downtown Port Coquitlam**, often called Downtown PoCo. The Wilson Avenue setting is part of a walkable city centre where parking, sidewalks, drop-offs and building entrances meet. That makes the transition between surfaces a useful place to start a winter site review.

The property’s supplied site plan has helped identify its snow-removal areas and pathways. That matters: a phrase such as “clear the parking lot” does not tell a crew everything about the path a patient or member of staff actually uses. A workable brief needs the included pedestrian connections as well as vehicle circulation.

For this renewed season, the written service scope remains the reference for the team and the property contact. A clear plan identifies the building’s own service areas and the connections between them.

## Medical-office snow removal experts look beyond the plow pass

Large open areas and narrow walking routes need different attention. At a medical-office property, we bring both into the planning conversation:

- **Parking-to-entrance connections:** establish the included route between arrival areas and the building doors.
- **Curbs and crossings:** identify the points where pedestrians and vehicles share or cross a route.
- **Entrance space:** plan the included landings, steps and any ramp as part of the access sequence.
- **Snow storage:** select workable placement areas without obstructing the agreed pedestrian routes.
- **Salting and changing conditions:** discuss ice-control work separately from the physical removal of snow.

The route people use is the point of the work. A cleared vehicle lane is only one part of that conversation. Site information, the agreed sequence and communication with the property team help keep attention on the access that matters.
