## BC Strata Property Act Liability — What Fairview Boards Need to Know

Under sections 3 and 72 of the *Strata Property Act*, a strata corporation is responsible for the repair and maintenance of common property. BC case law has repeatedly interpreted that duty to include keeping sidewalks, walkways, parkade ramps and entrance courts reasonably free of snow and ice during winter events.

What that means in practice for a Fairview strata council:

- **Documented service is the defense.** When a claim is filed, the strata's insurer asks for the service log — dispatch times, GPS breadcrumbs, salt-application photos, product used, technician on site. Without a written seasonal contract and per-visit documentation, the insurer's defense position collapses.
- **Per-event contractors create gaps.** A council paying "as needed" is implicitly deciding when service is needed. That decision, made by volunteers on a Sunday night, is exactly the exposure plaintiffs' counsel targets.
- **Seasonal contracts move the decision.** With a PlowWow seasonal contract, the trigger threshold is written down (typically 2 cm of accumulation or a forecast freezing-rain event) and dispatch is automatic. The contract, not the council, makes the live-event call.
- **Council minutes matter.** Every Fairview strata board should record in AGM minutes that a seasonal snow contract with defined response times was reviewed and approved. That paper trail is part of any future defense.

## Credentials to Verify Before Signing Any Fairview Contract

Before a strata council or commercial landlord in Fairview signs with anyone, verify:

- **WorkSafeBC (WCB) clearance letter.** No clearance letter, no contract. Under the *Workers Compensation Act*, the property owner can be assessed for a contractor's unpaid WCB premiums if a worker is injured on site.
- **Commercial general liability insurance.** Minimum $5,000,000, with the strata corporation or landlord named as additional insured. Ask for the certificate.
- **Automobile liability.** Every plow truck and salt unit needs commercial auto coverage.
- **Written service specification.** Trigger threshold in centimetres, response time from trigger, surfaces included, de-icer product, documentation delivered.
- **De-icer product disclosure.** Rock salt (NaCl), calcium chloride, magnesium chloride and calcium magnesium acetate each behave differently on pets, plants and concrete. Get the answer in writing.
- **Subcontractor policy.** Low-price bidders often route your site to whichever sub is closest that morning. Ask whether the crew is direct employees, and whether subs carry the same WCB and CGL coverage.
