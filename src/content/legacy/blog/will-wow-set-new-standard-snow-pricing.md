## A quote should start a clearer conversation

Will Wow set a new standard in snow removal pricing by making estimates easier to obtain through its website? That is the ambition worth testing. The useful change would be more than a fast number: a customer should understand what the number covers, which property details shaped it and what still requires review.

## Where the idea stands today

PlowWow has a private pricing worksheet in development. An instant public calculator is not live as part of this announcement; current website enquiries go through the quote-request process. Example worksheet figures are internal assumptions, not published tariffs or verified market averages.

## Show the included work beside the price

A seasonal figure should identify its covered period, service areas and snow and ice work. A per-visit figure should explain the visit being priced. Walkway length, parking areas, ramps and access restrictions deserve visible treatment. Customers should be able to distinguish included units from added units without reconstructing a hidden formula.

## Keep snow clearing and ice control distinct

A salting-only enquiry should not force a plowing charge. The treatment area, product allowance and ramp scope need their own explanation. A tool should also make minimum dispatch charges clear and avoid counting an included ramp or parking allowance twice. Those are design requirements for our proposed approach, not a promise that any surface or product suits every property.

## A seasonal price is not automatically unlimited

The written agreement must explain service triggers, response terms, allowances, extra work and exceptional-weather charges. Monthly instalments describe how a seasonal total is paid, not extra months of service. Estimated tax, rounding and the final payment should reconcile with the total. Clear terms matter as much as a prominent price.

## The standard will be judged after the estimate

The question remains open. A useful process should carry its assumptions into the reviewed quote, make changes visible and leave customers with a scope they can understand. Ask PlowWow about your property through the existing quote request. Availability, final scope and pricing require confirmation; an online enquiry is not a booking.
