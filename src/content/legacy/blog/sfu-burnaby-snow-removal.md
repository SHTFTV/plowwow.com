## Winter Weather Safety Tips

Always clear snow from your vehicle before driving to comply with BC laws. Avoid throwing snow onto roads or neighboring properties. Keep catch basins clear of snow and ice to prevent flooding. Pre-treat walkways with salt or sand before temperatures drop. Report any hazardous conditions to your strata council immediately. Maintain an emergency kit with flashlights and batteries.
