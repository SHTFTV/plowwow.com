Every East Vancouver neighbourhood claims to be "different." Renfrew-Collingwood actually is. Six specific conditions shape how snow has to be handled here:

## Vancouver Bylaw 2849 — What Every East Van Owner Needs to Know

Vancouver's Street & Traffic Bylaw 2849 places responsibility for clearing snow and ice from the sidewalk adjacent to any property on the property owner or occupier. The window is **10:00 a.m. the day following a snowfall** — meaning a Tuesday-night storm requires cleared sidewalks by 10:00 a.m. Wednesday.

In practice, that bylaw has three implications no Renfrew-Collingwood landlord or strata should ignore:

- **Fines are cumulative per property.** Multi-address strata complexes with multiple lot frontages can receive multiple tickets per event.
- **Vancouver is enforcing.** Bylaw officers actively patrol Kingsway and SkyTrain-station streets during snow events. This is not a paper rule.
- **Bylaw exposure is separate from civil liability.** A ticket is not the worst outcome. A slip-and-fall claim from a SkyTrain commuter on your frontage is.

Any Renfrew-Collingwood snow contract that treats public sidewalks as an "add-on" is under-scoped for this neighbourhood. PlowWow includes bylaw sidewalk clearing in every base scope.

## Strata Council Governance — What Matters for a Live Winter Event

Sections 3 and 72 of the BC *Strata Property Act* make the strata corporation responsible for common-property repair and maintenance. Case law has consistently interpreted that responsibility to include reasonable snow-and-ice management on entrance courts, walkways, ramps and shared surfaces.

For a Renfrew-Collingwood tower or walk-up strata, the practical council checklist looks like this:

- **Approve a seasonal contract at AGM, not on a snowy Sunday night.** Volunteer boards making live dispatch decisions during an event is the specific exposure pattern plaintiffs' counsel target.
- **Record the approval in minutes.** The minute is part of the eventual defence file.
- **Define the trigger in writing.** Typically 2 cm accumulation or a confirmed freezing-rain forecast. The trigger is not a phone call — it's a specification.
- **Require documentation deliverables.** GPS breadcrumbs, photos, product, rate, technician, time on site.
- **Renew before September 30.** Regional seasonal capacity fills through October.
