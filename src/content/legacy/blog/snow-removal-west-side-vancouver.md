## Winter Weather Tips for West Side Vancouver Residents

Stay safe and prepared this winter with our expert tips designed specifically for West Side Vancouver residents:

*   Ensure your vehicle is equipped with winter tires and emergency supplies.
*   Always clear snow from your vehicle before driving to comply with BC laws.
*   Avoid throwing snow onto roadways or neighboring properties.
*   Keep catch basins near your property free of snow and ice to prevent flooding.
*   Use de-icing products that are safe for natural stone and premium finishes.
*   Report hazardous conditions to your strata council as soon as possible.
*   Maintain a supply of emergency items, including backup power sources.
*   Familiarize yourself with your strata’s snow removal schedule and protocols.
*   Park in designated areas to facilitate snow removal equipment access.
*   Check on elderly neighbors during severe weather conditions.
*   Clear sidewalks promptly to uphold community standards.
*   Sign up for City of Vancouver weather alerts for timely updates.
*   Protect mature trees and premium landscaping during winter months.
*   Consider seasonal contracts for reliable and stress-free snow management.
