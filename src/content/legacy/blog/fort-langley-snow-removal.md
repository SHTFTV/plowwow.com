## Local Bylaws and Liability: What Fort Langley Property Owners Must Know

In the Township of Langley (ToL), snow removal isn't just a matter of convenience; it’s a legal requirement. According to **Township of Langley Highway and Traffic Bylaw 2023 No. 5800**, owners or occupiers of commercial, industrial, and multi-family residential properties must remove snow and ice from sidewalks bordering their property.

*   **Timeline:** Snow must often be cleared within 24 hours of a snowfall event.
*   **Prohibition:** You are prohibited from shoveling snow from your private property (driveways/sidewalks) onto the public roadway. Doing so can result in fines and creates a safety hazard for motorists and CN Rail crossings.
*   **Liability:** Under the BC Occupiers Liability Act, you have a "duty of care" to ensure that anyone entering your property is reasonably safe. Failure to manage ice can lead to costly legal battles.
