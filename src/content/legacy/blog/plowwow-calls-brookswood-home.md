## Winter service for Campbell Heights

For an industrial property, winter preparation starts with access. Delivery trucks need room to enter and turn. Loading areas need attention. Staff parking, building entrances and pedestrian routes all belong in the service plan.

PlowWow serves Campbell Heights businesses with snow and ice services planned around their properties. When arranging service, tell us about your delivery schedules, loading areas, gates and the routes people use to reach the building. Those details help us understand what needs attention and where the priorities are.

Whether you manage a warehouse, an industrial strata or a commercial yard, now is a good time to get that conversation started.

## From Brookswood to Greater Vancouver

Our Brookswood home base connects us to the communities we serve throughout Langley and South Surrey, while our service area extends across Greater Vancouver.

We work with homeowners, strata councils, property managers and businesses on winter service for:

- **Residential properties:** driveways, walkways and entrances.
- **Strata communities:** shared access, parking areas and pedestrian routes.
- **Commercial and industrial properties:** parking lots, loading areas, access lanes and building approaches.

The work starts with understanding your property and putting the service details into a clear plan. Your quote confirms the areas to be serviced, the work included and the arrangements for your property.
