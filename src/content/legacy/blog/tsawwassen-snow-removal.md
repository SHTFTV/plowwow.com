## Why Tsawwassen needs specialized snow & ice management

Does Tsawwassen need professional snow removal even with its mild climate? Yes, Tsawwassen requires specialized snow and ice management because its proximity to the Salish Sea creates high-humidity environments where temperatures frequently hover around the freezing mark, leading to dangerous "black ice" and heavy, moisture-laden snow.

While South Delta often sees less total accumulation than North Delta or the North Shore, the specific geography of the Tsawwassen peninsula presents unique challenges:

1.  **The Coastal Transition:** Cold outflow winds from the Fraser Valley often collide with moisture from the Strait of Georgia right over South Delta. This can lead to rapid accumulations that catch residents off guard.
2.  **Black Ice Risks:** Because our daytime temperatures often rise just above freezing and drop significantly at night, the "melt-refreeze" cycle is a constant threat. This creates invisible ice on sidewalks and driveways, particularly in shaded areas like English Bluff.
3.  **Salt-Sensitive Ecosystems:** Tsawwassen is home to the critical Boundary Bay migratory bird habitat and sensitive shoreline ecosystems. Professional management is required to ensure that de-icing agents are applied responsibly, preventing excessive runoff into protected waters.
4.  **Senior Safety:** With a higher-than-average senior population, maintaining clear, non-slip walkways is not just a matter of convenience; it is a critical safety requirement for mobility and emergency access.

## Local Bylaws and Liability: What Tsawwassen Property Owners Must Know

What are the snow removal bylaws in the City of Delta? According to the City of Delta (which governs Tsawwassen), property owners and occupiers are responsible for clearing snow and ice from sidewalks bordering their property by 10:00 a.m. each day, including Sundays.

Failure to comply with these bylaws can result in fines and, more importantly, significant legal liability. If a pedestrian slips on an icy sidewalk in front of your home or business, you could be held responsible for damages.

*   **Commercial Obligations:** Businesses must ensure that all exits, entrances, and handicap parking stalls are clear for public use.
*   **Strata Responsibilities:** Strata councils have a duty of care to ensure that all common areas are safe for residents and visitors. This includes internal walkways, parking lots, and the sidewalks surrounding the property.
*   **Residential Requirements:** Even if you are away on vacation, the bylaw requires the sidewalk to be cleared. PlowWow specializes in "worry-free" contracts that trigger automatically based on accumulation, so you never have to worry about a fine or a neighbor’s safety.
