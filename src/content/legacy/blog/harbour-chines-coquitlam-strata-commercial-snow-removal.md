## Harbour Chines' Snow Profile — Why This Corner of Coquitlam Is Different

Harbour Chines isn't a flat Coquitlam grid. Its elevation, aspect and street layout produce a distinct set of winter risks:

- **Elevation-driven cold pool.** Harbour Chines climbs from roughly 90 to 160 metres between Como Lake and the ridgeline. Overnight temperatures at the top run 2–3 °C colder than Coquitlam Centre.
- **Sloped side streets.** Foster, Robinson, Grover and Winslow all fall north-to-south on grades between 6% and 10%. A cleared road at 6 a.m. can be glaze by 8 a.m.
- **North-facing driveways.** Split-level estate driveways carved into the hill are shaded through winter. The bottom of every one holds ice long after the road is dry.
- **Townhome strata mews.** The complexes along Harbour Chines Drive and Austin have narrow shared mews with concrete stairs that municipal plows never touch.
- **Austin Avenue commercial frontage.** Long uninterrupted commercial sidewalks between Blue Mountain and Robinson generate high bylaw-clearing exposure per lineal foot.
- **Como Lake Park pedestrian tributaries.** Residents walk to the lake on the same sidewalks that fill with black ice on refreeze mornings.

## Hillside Landlord & Strata Liability — Occupiers Liability Act and Coquitlam Bylaw Duty

Harbour Chines property owners carry two overlapping duties every Coquitlam winter.

Under the *Occupiers Liability Act* (BC), an occupier owes a duty to take that care that in all the circumstances of the case is reasonable to see that a person on the premises will be reasonably safe. In hillside strata and estate case law, "reasonable" has meant a written, pre-morning-commute clearing protocol on every walkway a resident, visitor or tenant uses.

Under City of Coquitlam Bylaw No. 3939 (Sidewalk Snow and Ice Removal), property owners must clear public sidewalks adjacent to their property within 24 hours of a snowfall. Austin Avenue and Como Lake Avenue commercial frontages are the most-ticketed corridors in west Coquitlam.

What this means for a Harbour Chines strata council, estate owner or landlord:

- **The 24-hour bylaw window is a floor.** Commercial sites need to be clear by first light. Residential stratas need to be clear before residents leave for work.
- **Photo documentation isn't optional.** Slip-and-fall plaintiffs subpoena the service log.
- **Sloped surfaces need explicit scope.** Any contract that says "walkways" without naming stairs, ramps and stair landings is under-scoped for Harbour Chines.
