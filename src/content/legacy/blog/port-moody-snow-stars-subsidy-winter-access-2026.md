## Is Port Moody offering the new $450 snow-removal payment?

**We have not verified an approved Port Moody version of the Peterborough pilot.** Snow Stars is a neighbour-recognition program, not evidence of a municipal cash benefit or a guaranteed clearing appointment. Ask the City about current assistance before relying on funding.

Peterborough’s [pilot subsidy](https://www.peterborough.ca/living-in-peterborough/roads-and-sidewalks/snow-removal/pilot-snow-removal-subsidy-program/) offers up to $450 for qualifying private snow-removal costs. Its two payments fall in November 2026 and February 2027, with receipts required for the second. A local proposal would need its own design.

The [October 8 Kawarthanow report](https://kawarthanow.com/2026/10/08/applications-open-october-15-for-city-of-peterboroughs-pilot-snow-removal-subsidy-program/) raises a useful Port Moody question: could limited financial assistance complement neighbour help without asking volunteers to act like an on-call contractor?

## What Snow Stars does—and what to confirm separately

Port Moody’s [published winter guidance](https://www.portmoody.ca/news-and-notices/posts/are-you-winter-ready-let-s-work-together-to-keep-roads-and-sidewalks-clear-and-safe-in-snowy-weather/) invites residents to nominate a helpful neighbour as a Snow Star. The recognition celebrates assistance already given. Do not interpret a nomination as a booking, dispatch service or subsidy application.

The same guidance distinguishes sidewalk timing: 10 a.m. for Priority Pedestrian Routes and within 24 hours for other routes. It identifies pedestrian areas around Inlet Centre and Moody Centre stations in the City’s priority system. Check the actual route and responsible party using the City’s maps; station proximity does not transfer a private frontage obligation to a municipal crew.

The page is dated December 2025. Use it as the published guidance reviewed for this article, and ask the City to confirm any changes for 2026–27. Questions about snow and ice response can go through its online reporting route or **604-469-4574**.

## Why recognition and reliable coverage need different plans

A neighbour may willingly help after work but be unable to attend during every snowfall. A service agreement can specify surfaces, timing and return visits, but it also depends on actual provider capacity. A possible pilot should describe which need it is trying to meet before assigning money or volunteer hours.

For a hypothetical home on an inclined approach, useful assistance might focus on the entrance and connection to the sidewalk. For a strata building, the priority may be a continuous path between entrances, parking and frontage. Those are different work requests, with different people authorized to arrange them.

Council could be asked to assess a small paid backup for residents who cannot arrange dependable help. The design should ask how access restrictions, steps and treatment needs affect the quote. Copying another municipality’s dollar figure without defining the work would leave residents and providers making different assumptions.

## Four practical questions for Port Moody council

Use the official [Council contact page](https://www.portmoody.ca/city-government/council/) for current contact information. A focused request could ask:

- What help is available when a resident cannot clear snow and has no reliable neighbour assistance?
- Would staff assess a funded trial alongside existing community recognition?
- How would an applicant describe the essential route and get a suitable service quote?
- How would the City measure completed work and identify needs the trial could not cover?

Keep a policy request about service gaps and outcomes. Ask staff for a private process if financial or disability documentation is ever needed. This article is not asking residents to send personal records to PlowWow.

## Connect the building plan to the public route

For a strata or business, mark the frontage, entry ramp, curb connection, gate and parking-to-door route on one plan. Confirm ownership or maintenance responsibility at each boundary. A public path toward a station or park may have a different service schedule from the building’s private walkway.

Ask the contractor how it will keep access workable while clearing the vehicle area. Include narrow passages that need hand work and snow-storage locations that do not obstruct the pedestrian route. Our [Port Moody service page](/port-moody) and [strata snow-plowing guide](/port-moody-strata-commercial-snow-plowing) help turn that plan into a service enquiry.

## Book around defined work, not an unconfirmed rebate

A [PlowWow quote request](/quote) should include the address, surfaces, dimensions, photos and required access times. Ask about seasonal service, five visits or a single attendance, subject to availability. Specify whether the request includes snow clearing, salting, suitable brine treatment or plowing with salt, and agree what triggers another visit.

Do not deduct a hypothetical grant from the contract price. PlowWow is a commercial service contact, not a municipal program administrator or an approved provider under a program that has not been confirmed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does a Snow Star nomination reserve snow clearing?

No. The cited City information describes recognition for helpful neighbours, not a service reservation.

### Would a future subsidy replace a strata’s winter contract?

That cannot be assumed. Any new program would define eligibility and covered work; shared-property maintenance still needs its own plan.
