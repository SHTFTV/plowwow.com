## Navigating the Legal Landscape: Liability and Compliance

The legal stakes for Surrey property managers have never been higher. ## Overcoming Common Operational Pitfalls: Parkades and Catch Basins

Surrey's architectural landscape often includes aging underground parkades and complex drainage systems. These features present two of the most significant winter hazards: **Parkade Ramp Icing** and **Catch Basin Freeze-Thaw Blocks**.

**1. Parkade Ramps:** The transition from the warm interior of a parkade to the freezing exterior ramp is a prime location for black ice. Our site maps specifically target these "Transition Zones" for high-frequency salting.

**2. Catch Basins:** When snow melts and refreezes, catch basins become clogged with ice. If a second storm hits, the meltwater has nowhere to go, causing localized flooding in the strata parking lot, which then turns into an "ice rink" as temperatures drop.
