## BC Strata Property Act Liability — What Cedar Cottage Boards Need to Know

Under sections 3 and 72 of the *Strata Property Act*, a strata corporation is responsible for the repair and maintenance of common property. BC case law has repeatedly read that duty to include keeping sidewalks, walkways, parkade ramps and entrance courts reasonably free of snow and ice during winter events.

In practice, for a Kensington-Cedar Cottage strata council:

- **Documented service is the defense.** When a claim is filed, the insurer asks for the service log — dispatch times, GPS breadcrumbs, salt-application photos, product used, technician on site. Without a written seasonal contract and per-visit documentation, the defense position collapses.
- **Per-event contractors create gaps.** Paying "as needed" means the council decides when service is needed. That volunteer decision, made on a Sunday night, is exactly the exposure plaintiffs' counsel targets.
- **Seasonal contracts move the decision.** With a PlowWow seasonal contract, the trigger threshold is written (typically 2 cm of accumulation or a forecast freezing-rain event) and dispatch is automatic. The contract makes the live-event call, not the council.
- **Council minutes matter.** Record in AGM minutes that a seasonal snow contract with defined response times was reviewed and approved. That paper trail is part of any future defense.

## Credentials to Verify Before Signing Any Contract Here

Before a strata council or commercial landlord signs with anyone, verify:

- **WorkSafeBC (WCB) clearance letter.** No clearance letter, no contract. Under the *Workers Compensation Act*, the property owner can be assessed for a contractor's unpaid WCB premiums if a worker is injured on site.
- **Commercial general liability insurance.** Minimum $5,000,000, with the strata corporation or landlord named as additional insured. Ask for the certificate.
- **Automobile liability.** Every plow truck and salt unit needs commercial auto coverage.
- **Written service specification.** Trigger threshold in centimetres, response time from trigger, surfaces included, de-icer product, documentation delivered.
- **De-icer product disclosure.** Rock salt (NaCl), calcium chloride, magnesium chloride and calcium magnesium acetate each behave differently on pets, plants and concrete. Get it in writing.
- **Subcontractor policy.** Ask whether the crew on your site will be direct employees or subs — and whether subs carry the same WCB and CGL coverage.
