Most of these strip malls share a similar physical profile — modest customer lot in front, storefront sidewalk running the length of the building, loading and tenant parking in the rear, often a small drive-through or rear-access lane.

- Drive aisles and parking stalls need to be defined — buried sightlines cause accidents.
- Snow stacking needs to avoid blocking storefront visibility, signage, and pedestrian crossings.
