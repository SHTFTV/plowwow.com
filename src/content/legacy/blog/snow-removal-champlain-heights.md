Winter in Champlain Heights can be challenging, but with the right preparation, you can stay safe and comfortable. Here are some expert tips to help you navigate the season:

*   Ensure your vehicle is equipped with winter tires and an emergency kit.
*   Regularly clear snow from your vehicle before driving to avoid fines and ensure visibility.
*   Refrain from shoveling snow onto roads or neighboring properties to prevent hazards.
*   Keep storm drains near your property clear to prevent flooding.
*   Pre-treat walkways with salt or sand before freezing temperatures set in.
*   Report any hazardous conditions to your strata council immediately.
*   Maintain an emergency supply kit, including flashlights, batteries, and non-perishable food.
*   Familiarize yourself with your strata’s snow removal schedule and procedures.
*   Park in designated areas to allow snow removal equipment access.
*   Check on elderly or vulnerable neighbors during severe weather conditions.
*   Clear sidewalks promptly to comply with city regulations.
*   Sign up for local weather alerts to stay informed about upcoming snow events.
