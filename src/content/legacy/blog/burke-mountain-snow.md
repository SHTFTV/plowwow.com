#### Essential Winter Weather Tips for Burke Mountain Residents

Prepare for the winter season with these expert tips to keep you and your family safe:

1. Equip your vehicle with winter tires and keep emergency supplies handy, including blankets, food, and water.

2. Always clear snow from your vehicle before driving to comply with BC laws and ensure visibility.

3. Avoid throwing snow onto roadways or neighboring properties to prevent hazards.

4. Use salt or sand on walkways before temperatures drop to prevent ice formation.

5. Report any hazardous conditions to your strata council promptly.

6. Stay informed by signing up for mountain weather alerts and plan alternate routes for emergencies.

7. Check on elderly neighbors during heavy snowfalls to ensure their safety.

8. Park vehicles to allow snow removal equipment access on narrow streets.

9. Familiarize yourself with your strata’s snow removal schedule to avoid surprises.

10. Keep extra emergency supplies due to potential road closures in severe weather.
