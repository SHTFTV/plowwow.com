## Annual contracts give priority service a plan

A group of nearby properties is easier to service consistently than a route stretched across unrelated cities. That is why we are focusing Richmond bookings by area, access requirements and the time each property needs attention.

An annual agreement reserves the winter-service arrangement defined in your contract. It should state the covered season, service triggers, priority areas, pricing structure and follow-up responsibilities. Annual does not automatically mean unlimited visits, an all-inclusive fixed price or year-round grounds maintenance. We will put the actual arrangement in writing.

If you already have a contractor for another service, tell us what is covered. We can discuss the winter work you need without leaving a gap or duplicating someone else’s scope.

## Our five-hour post-storm service standard

PlowWow plans its routes around a five-hour service standard after a storm event ends. To make that useful for your property, the agreement needs to define the event-end trigger, the work included in the window and any access or operating requirements.

Tell us when shops open, staff arrive, residents leave and deliveries begin. A busy strip mall may need a different sequence from a strata parkade entrance. For prolonged or repeated snowfall, agree on how interim visits and the final post-storm service will be handled. Frost, freezing rain and refreezing also need their own treatment and inspection scope.

Priority booking means confirmed route capacity and an agreed plan. A quote request alone does not reserve a place or promise an individual arrival time.

## White-glove treatment for a working property

White-glove service means paying attention to the details your customers, tenants and residents use every day. During the property review, we will work through the areas that matter:

- Vehicle entrances, circulation lanes and the parking areas included in the contract.
- Storefront approaches, accessible parking connections, stairs and pedestrian routes where hand clearing is requested.
- Loading areas, waste collection access and places that must remain available.
- Snow-storage locations that avoid blocking access or directing meltwater toward entrances.
- Curbs, landscaping, drain covers, decorative paving and sensitive concrete that crews need to recognize.
- Approved salting or ice-control treatments, reporting expectations and the contact for site concerns.

Plowing the lot and looking after pedestrian access are related jobs with different equipment and time requirements. We will identify both clearly. Snow relocation or hauling, extra visits and work outside the agreed areas should also be confirmed rather than assumed.

## Help us match your property to the Richmond route

Send the property address, a site plan or marked photos, approximate lot size and the areas you want serviced. Flag new or stamped concrete and any installer restrictions before we choose clearing methods or materials. If you have had trouble with drifting, pooling water or shaded ice in previous winters, point out those locations too.
