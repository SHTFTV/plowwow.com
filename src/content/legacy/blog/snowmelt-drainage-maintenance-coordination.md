## One concern can involve several teams

A puddle near an entrance may be reported during a snow-service visit, but that does not make every drainage task part of the snow contract. Start by identifying the observation, the immediate concern and the person responsible for deciding what happens next.

Snow clearing, drain maintenance, plumbing and repairs can require separate scopes. Keep those distinctions visible so an unresolved maintenance item does not disappear inside a general request to deal with the snow.

## Map known collection points

Ask the property team where water has collected during previous winters. Mark those locations on the working site plan together with the entrances and walking routes they affect. The purpose is to help people recognize and report changing conditions.

A diagram should not assert the direction of an underground drain or identify a blockage without evidence. Use labels such as observed low spot or reported pooling until the appropriate person assesses the cause.

## Consider snow placement alongside runoff

Discuss candidate storage areas with the provider and the person responsible for site maintenance. Include known drains, pedestrian connections and other constraints in that conversation. An empty corner is not automatically a suitable storage location.

Environment and Climate Change Canada identifies snow disposal and salt management as related environmental-management topics for road authorities. That broader guidance is a reason to discuss runoff thoughtfully; it is not a drainage design for a private property.

## Decide who receives each report

A useful winter plan names the contact for snow-service observations and the contact for building maintenance. If the same person fills both roles, still record which action they are considering and what approval is needed.

Keep visit notes factual and preserve their dates. If a specialist is asked to investigate drainage, retain that separate report rather than rewriting an earlier observation as though its cause had already been confirmed.

## Prepare a coordinated service conversation

Bring a site sketch, a description of recurring conditions and the appropriate maintenance contact to the discussion. Confirm what the snow-service provider will report and which work requires another arrangement.
