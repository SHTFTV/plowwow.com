## 2. Navigating the Legal Landscape: The BC Occupiers Liability Act and Chilliwack Bylaws

For a strata council, an unmanaged sidewalk or an un-sanded parking lot is a significant liability risk. Under the British Columbia Occupiers Liability Act, a strata corporation owes a statutory duty of care to ensure that all residents, visitors, and delivery personnel are reasonably safe while utilizing the common property.

### Legal Definitions and Strata Exposure

"Common property" encompasses everything outside an individual townhome or condo door, including:

- Internal roadways and visitor parking bays.
- Concrete walkways, mail kiosk access points, and garbage enclosures.
- Underground parkade ramps (which are highly susceptible to tracking ice).

If a resident slips on an untreated patch of black ice at 7:00 AM while walking to their vehicle, the strata corporation can be held legally liable for all resulting medical damages, lost wages, and legal fees. In recent years, BC courts have increasingly penalized stratas that cannot produce verifiable, time-stamped proof of a proactive snow and ice management plan.

### Chilliwack Municipal Compliance

In addition to provincial civil liability, the City of Chilliwack enforces strict regulations regarding sidewalk clearing. Commercial properties and strata complexes must clear snow and ice from all public walkways bordering the property within 24 hours of a snowfall event. Failure to comply results in municipal fines, and more importantly, establishes a documented record of negligence that can be used against the strata in a civil lawsuit.

## 5. Overcoming Common Operational Pitfalls: Underground Ramps and Catch Basins

A significant portion of strata property damage occurs due to a lack of planning regarding micro-zones on the property. Two areas that demand professional oversight are underground parkade ramps and catch basin drainage zones.

### The Underground Parkade Hazard

Underground parking ramps are a notorious risk zone for strata complexes. As vehicles enter from the outside, they track snow, slush, and moisture onto the cold concrete ramp. Because these structures are shielded from direct sunlight and exposed to freezing air currents, this moisture instantly transitions into smooth, clear ice. Vehicles lose traction trying to exit, sliding backward into walls or security gates.

plowwow.com addresses this by implementing a dedicated treatment protocol for all covered and open ramps. We apply localized, high-concentration eco-friendly de-icing solutions that lower the freezing point of water well below standard rock salt capabilities, ensuring your residents maintain maximum traction even on steep inclines.

```
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                  PARKADE RAMP & DRAINAGE MITIGATION                     |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| [Tracked Snow/Slush] -> [Freezing Air Currents] -> [Clear Ice Hazard]   |
|                                                                         |
|  PLOWWOW SOLUTION:                                                      |
|  - Pre-treatment with high-concentration liquid de-icers on ramps       |
|  - Clearing catch basins post-plow to prevent freeze-thaw pooling       |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### The Catch Basin Freeze-Thaw Trap

Another common pitfall is improper snow stacking over catch basins and storm drains. When temperatures rise slightly during the day, the snow pile begins to melt. If the meltwater cannot reach a cleared drain, it pools across the parking lot. As night falls and temperatures plunge back below zero, this pooling water transforms into a massive sheet of black ice.

Our operators are trained to never stack snow on top of or directly adjacent to catch basins. We ensure that drainage paths remain clear so that melting runoff flows off your property, preventing dangerous freeze-thaw cycles.
