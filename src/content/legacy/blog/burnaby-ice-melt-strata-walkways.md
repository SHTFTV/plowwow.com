# Burnaby ice melt: what to check before treating a strata walkway

![PlowWow winter walkway planning in Burnaby](/blog-images/burnaby-strata-winter-walkways-park-access.jpg)

**By the PlowWow team · November 20, 2026**

On a cold morning, the most useful question for a Burnaby strata is often a small one: what is happening on the stretch between the accessible parking stall and the front door? A dry entrance can sit just metres from a ramp where yesterday’s runoff has frozen.

## How cold has Burnaby Mountain been?

Environment and Climate Change Canada’s Burnaby Simon Fraser U station recorded **−14.0°C on November 27, 1985**, and **−19.4°C on December 29, 1968**. Those are the November and December extremes in that station’s published 1965–2007 extreme-temperature series. Each number describes air temperature at one station. Neither tells a caretaker the pavement temperature at a Kingsway entrance this morning. [See the station table and observation period](https://climate.weather.gc.ca/climate_normals/results_1981_2010_e.html?climate_id=1101158).

## Before putting down ice melt

Clear loose snow first so you can see the surface. Follow the walking route past the door: look at the shaded turn, the bottom of the ramp and anywhere water crosses the path. Salt’s melting performance changes with pavement temperature. Check the product’s labelled working range, application rate and surface restrictions before deciding how to treat that spot. If a route remains slippery, alert the building contact, identify an alternate route where available and arrange a return check. [How pavement temperature affects deicing](https://iowadot.gov/modes-travel/roads-highways/maintenance/winter-operations/roadway-deicing).

## Use the QR sign for the right building

Where a PlowWow building sign is installed, scan its QR code and confirm the address on the property page. Two neighbouring buildings can have different crews or access instructions. A message such as “ice at the foot of the east parkade ramp, beside stall 12, seen at 7:15 a.m.” gives the team a location and time to check. Review and send the text from your messaging app. [How PlowWow’s building-specific QR signs work](/snow-intelligence-property-qr-signs-kingsway-metro-vancouver).
