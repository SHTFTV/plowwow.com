## Local bylaws and liability — what Squamish property owners must know

The District of Squamish has specific bylaws regarding snow and ice removal that property owners must follow to avoid fines and mitigate liability.

According to the **District of Squamish Traffic Bylaw**, owners or occupiers of real property must remove snow and ice from sidewalks bordering their property no later than 10:00 a.m. on any day that snow has accumulated. This applies to both residential and commercial properties. Failure to do so can result in municipal fines and, more significantly, opens the property owner to civil liability if a pedestrian is injured.

Furthermore, it is prohibited to plow or shovel snow from private property onto public roads or sidewalks. This common mistake can cause drainage issues and visibility hazards for drivers on Highway 99 or local arterials like Mamquam Road. PlowWow’s professional operators are trained in "snow stacking" techniques that keep your property compliant with all local bylaws while maintaining maximum parking capacity.
