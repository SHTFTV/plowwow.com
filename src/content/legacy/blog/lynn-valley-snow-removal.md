## Why Lynn Valley needs specialized snow & ice management

Lynn Valley requires specialized snow and ice management because its high elevation and proximity to the North Shore mountains create a localized climate where snow accumulation is significantly higher than in Lower Lonsdale or Vancouver. While it may be raining at the Lonsdale Quay, the "rain-snow line" frequently sits right at the intersection of Mountain Highway and Lynn Valley Road, turning the neighborhood into a winter landscape in minutes.

The topography of Lynn Valley presents several challenges that standard snow removal providers often struggle to manage:

1.  **The Rain-Snow Line:** The elevation gain from the highway up to the top of Lynn Valley Road (near Kilmer Park) can be over 100 metres. This often results in heavy, wet "heart-attack snow" that is difficult to shovel and quick to turn into a sheet of ice as temperatures drop overnight.
2.  **Steep Terrains:** Streets like Frederick Road and Underwood Avenue feature significant gradients. Without professional Lynn Valley ice control and high-traction equipment, these roads can become impassable for standard vehicles.
3.  **The Lynn Creek Watershed:** Environmental stewardship is vital in our community. Because we are situated near Lynn Canyon Park and the sensitive Lynn Creek watershed, snow and ice management must be handled with precise salt application to prevent excessive runoff into local ecosystems.
4.  **Heavy Accumulations:** It is not uncommon for Lynn Valley to receive 10–15 centimeters more snow than coastal areas during a single weather event. This requires a "first-on-the-scene" approach to prevent snow from compacting into ice.

## Local bylaws and liability — what Lynn Valley property owners must know

Property owners in Lynn Valley are legally required by the District of North Vancouver (DNV) to clear snow and ice from sidewalks bordering their property. According to DNV Bylaw 4299, residential and commercial owners must clear snow from sidewalks within 24 hours of a snowfall, though in high-traffic areas near Lynn Valley Centre, much faster action is expected for public safety.

Failure to comply can result in fines, but the greater risk is civil liability. If a pedestrian slips on an icy patch in front of your home near Argyle Secondary or a bus stop on 27th Street, the property owner may be held liable for damages. PlowWow takes this burden off your shoulders by providing automated, documented snow clearing that meets and exceeds municipal standards.
