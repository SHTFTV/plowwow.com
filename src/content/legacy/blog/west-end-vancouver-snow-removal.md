## Why West End Vancouver Snow Removal Is Different

The West End is not a suburb. Between Stanley Park, Burrard, Georgia and Beach Avenue, you have roughly 220 mid- and high-rise buildings packed into 2 square kilometres. That density changes every part of snow removal:

- **Ramps, not driveways.** Almost every West End strata has a below-grade parkade with a sloped ramp — Robson, Nicola, Cardero, Bidwell. When those ramps ice over, residents are trapped and delivery vehicles can't enter.
- **Sidewalks are the front yard.** The City of Vancouver Street and Traffic By-law requires occupiers to clear adjacent public sidewalks by **10 a.m. the day after a snowfall**. In the West End, that's your entire block of Davie, Denman or Robson frontage.
- **No on-site snow storage.** There is nowhere to push snow. Piles have to be strategically placed against curbs or trucked out.
- **Pet density is enormous.** The West End has the highest dog-per-capita rate in Vancouver. Rock salt (sodium chloride) burns paws. Pet-safe de-icer is not a nice-to-have — it's a bylaw complaint waiting to happen.
