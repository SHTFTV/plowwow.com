## Price the slope and the access, not just the driveway

For a Coquitlam property, tell us whether the driveway, entrance or parkade approach is sloped. A short steep route and a longer level route are different work descriptions. Identify any connecting steps, landings and gates, and show where a machine could turn or where hand clearing is needed. The beta does not automatically assess a slope from your address or add a verified elevation surcharge.

## A house scope and a strata scope are different

A standard single driveway and walkway can start with the residential example below. A multi-building site needs its common pedestrian route, parking circulation and ramp considered separately. Unit count alone is not a measurement of the work. Individual-unit stairs and private paths are excluded unless agreed; connecting stairs on an agreed common route should be identified in the proposal.

## Use Coquitlam’s rule when planning the service

Coquitlam’s Boulevard Maintenance Bylaw requires the relevant snow and ice removal within ten hours of the cessation of a snowfall or ice storm. The service plan needs to address the property’s actual obligations and the agreed response terms. A calculator result does not establish that a crew can meet your site’s required timing.

## Compare an ordinary visit with the full winter plan

For 2026/27, decide whether you want a seasonal arrangement, a five-visit booking or individual service. Ask what is included in each: triggers, material, repeat visits and heavy or prolonged snowfall terms. Do not multiply one advertised visit price by a guessed number of storms and assume it is a seasonal contract. A storm can involve more than one clearing pass and additional ice-control work.
