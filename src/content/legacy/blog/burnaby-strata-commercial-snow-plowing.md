## Navigating the Legal Landscape: BC Occupiers Liability Act & Bylaws

The legal framework for snow removal in Burnaby is governed by two main pillars: the **BC Occupiers Liability Act** and **City of Burnaby Bylaws**. Under the provincial Act, property "occupiers" (which includes strata corporations and commercial landlords) have a "duty of care" to ensure that anyone entering the property is reasonably safe.

In Burnaby specifically, bylaws require that snow, ice, and slush be removed from sidewalks surrounding commercial and multi-family properties by **10:00 AM every day**, including holidays. Failure to comply can result in municipal fines, but the greater risk is the civil liability of a slip-and-fall claim, which can reach seven figures.

## Overcoming Common Operational Pitfalls

Two of the most common failures in Burnaby winter maintenance occur at the "invisible" parts of the property: **Underground Parkade Ramps** and **Catch Basins**.

Because many Burnaby stratas are built on slopes, parkade ramps become "ice chutes" if not treated with liquid brine. Furthermore, the "freeze-thaw" cycle—common in Burnaby's 0°C to +3°C hovering window—leads to catch basin blockages. When snow melts during the day and freezes at night, a blocked drain creates a localized skating rink.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How does plowwow.com handle "Black Ice" in Metrotown/High-Traffic areas?
Black ice is our primary target. In high-traffic Burnaby areas, we utilize a combination of pre-emptive salting and liquid de-icers. Since black ice often forms when ambient temperatures are above freezing but the pavement is below freezing, our trucks are equipped with infrared pavement sensors to trigger treatment before the ice even forms.

### What is the Burnaby bylaw regarding snow removal times?
The City of Burnaby Street and Traffic Bylaw requires all owners or occupiers of multi-family, commercial, and industrial properties to remove all snow and ice from the sidewalks bordering their property no later than 10:00 AM every day. **plowwow.com** routes are specifically designed to ensure all "Bylaw Critical" zones are cleared well before this deadline.
