## Why Dunbar Is a Different Snow-Removal Job Than the Rest of Vancouver

Dunbar-Southlands sits on the south-west shoulder of Vancouver, bounded by Pacific Spirit Park to the west, the Fraser River and Musqueam lands to the south, and Arbutus Ridge to the east. It's a neighborhood of restored 1920s and 1930s character homes, mature double-lot estates near Southlands, walk-up rental apartments along Dunbar Street, and a tight commercial corridor on West 41st Avenue between Dunbar and Crown. Every one of those property types behaves differently in a snow event, and a snow contractor who only services strata or only services single-family homes will get at least one of them wrong.

PlowWow has built our Dunbar service around the neighborhood's three signature challenges: (1) character-home stairs and stone walkways that cannot tolerate rock salt, (2) the West 41st commercial corridor that needs walkways cleared and salted before the 7:00 AM open, and (3) the Southlands-adjacent estates with long gravel driveways, equestrian access lanes, and large dog-friendly outdoor envelopes that need pet-safe chemistry across the board.

## Microclimate: Why Dunbar Holds Snow Longer Than Kitsilano

Even though Dunbar is only 4 km south of Kitsilano, it consistently records more accumulation and longer snow-on-ground duration each winter. Two reasons. First, Dunbar sits 40–60 metres above sea level on the south slope of the Vancouver Peninsula, so it's slightly cooler and gets the leading edge of any south-flowing arctic air mass from the Fraser Valley. Second, the dense mature tree canopy along Dunbar Street, Crown Street, and the side streets feeding into Pacific Spirit Park shades sidewalks until late morning, slowing natural melt by 2–3 hours compared to more open neighborhoods.

The practical effect: a 6 cm overnight snowfall that's gone from Kits by lunchtime will still be on Dunbar sidewalks at 3:00 PM. Property owners who try to wait it out frequently miss the City of Vancouver's 10:00 AM clearing deadline (Street and Traffic By-law 2849, Section 76) and risk both the $250 fine and the liability exposure for any slip-and-fall on their unsalted sidewalk frontage.

## Character Homes: Why Chemistry Matters

The character-home stock in Dunbar is one of the best-preserved in Vancouver. Original sandstone foundations, lead-flashed brick stairs, slate front walkways, and cast-iron railings are common. Every one of those materials is damaged by sodium chloride (rock salt). Salt-driven efflorescence on sandstone foundations and mortar deterioration on brick stairs are expensive heritage repairs — often $400–$1,800 per linear metre.

PlowWow uses a stair-and-walkway chemistry program specifically for Dunbar character homes:

* Pre-storm: traction sand applied to stone stairs and slate walkways
* Active snow event: mechanical clearing with insulated grain shovels and single-stage battery blowers (no salt)
* Post-storm: calcium-magnesium-acetate (CMA) on the city sidewalk frontage and potassium-formate pet-safe granules at the front entry and side doors
* Refreeze events: a magnesium-chloride brine spot-applied only to icy patches, never broadcast

This program adds approximately 18% to the per-event cost compared to a rock-salt approach, but it eliminates the heritage-repair risk. Every Dunbar character-home contract includes this chemistry by default.

No. Rock salt damages sandstone, brick mortar, and slate. Dunbar character-home contracts use traction sand pre-storm, mechanical clearing during the event, and pet-safe potassium-formate granules at the entry — never sodium chloride on the stairs themselves.
