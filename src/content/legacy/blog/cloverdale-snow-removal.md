## Local bylaws and liability — what Cloverdale property owners must know

In the City of Surrey, snow removal is not just a courtesy—it is a legal requirement. According to the **Surrey Property Maintenance and Occupancy Bylaw**, owners or occupiers of commercial or industrial premises must remove snow and ice from sidewalks bordering their property by 10:00 AM every day, including Sundays.

For residential owners, while the bylaws focus heavily on the safety of the public right-of-way, the liability risks are significant. As a property owner in Cloverdale, you are responsible for maintaining a "reasonably safe" environment for visitors, delivery drivers, and mail carriers. Failure to clear ice can result in fines from the city or, worse, personal injury lawsuits.

PlowWow helps you stay compliant by ensuring that all perimeter sidewalks and entranceways are cleared according to municipal standards. Our team stays updated on all Surrey bylaws to ensure your property never falls under the scrutiny of bylaw enforcement officers.
