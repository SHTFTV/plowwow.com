## A preparation cue, not a city snow forecast

CTV News reported on October 28 that meteorologists are watching a possible early weakening of the polar vortex, with the outcome for Canada unresolved. That is a reason to review winter readiness, not a prediction of a particular storm. This Burnaby guide is an original planning checklist. It does not predict local snowfall, a start date or the severity of this winter.

## Make shared areas unambiguous

The City of Burnaby asks residents and businesses to keep sidewalks free of snow and ice. For a strata, turn that general responsibility into a marked property plan: adjoining sidewalks, common paths, entrances and any area excluded from the proposed service. Check the city’s current page rather than borrowing another municipality’s rule.

## Choose the person who can make the call

Council members, a manager, a caretaker and a contractor may all have information. They should not have to guess who can approve a change. Record a primary contact, a backup and the agreed channel for urgent operational questions. Confirm who can authorise additional treatment or a return visit.

![Original planning strip: MAP, AUTHORISE, CONFIRM](/blog-images/polar-vortex-burnaby-strata-winter-handoff-strip.jpg)

## Separate a report from a completed task

A resident’s report identifies a concern. An instruction authorises action. A completion record describes work actually performed. Keep those stages distinct when discussing service. A text saying that an area needs attention is not evidence that a contractor has attended it.

## Review the route as people use it

Look at the connection between buildings, visitor parking and common entrances. Identify stairs, ramps and places where a machine cannot work. Agree where snow may be placed without blocking access. This is a practical review framework, not an inspection of a particular Burnaby strata or a claim that PlowWow already serves it.

## Use a current forecast for the actual decision

ECCC’s seasonal guidance concerns longer-period probabilities, not an address-specific attendance time. Recheck local forecasts and warnings as an event approaches. Ask PlowWow to review the property and confirm scope, availability and commercial terms before relying on a service arrangement.
