### Essential Winter Safety Tips for East Vancouver Residents

Stay prepared this winter with our expert tips:

*   Ensure your vehicle is equipped with winter tires and an emergency kit.
*   Clear snow off your car before driving to comply with local regulations.
*   Avoid piling snow onto streets or neighboring properties.
*   Regularly check and clear catch basins near your home.
*   Pre-treat walkways with salt or sand before freezing temperatures set in.
*   Report any hazardous conditions to your strata council immediately.
*   Keep a supply of flashlights and batteries for emergencies.
*   Familiarize yourself with your strata’s snow removal schedule and procedures.
*   Park in designated areas to allow snow removal equipment access.
*   Look out for elderly neighbors during severe weather conditions.
*   Clear sidewalks promptly to adhere to city bylaws.
*   Subscribe to City of Vancouver weather alerts for timely updates.
*   Be cautious of cyclists and pedestrians in this walkable community.
*   Ensure back lane access is clear for garbage collection services.
*   Support local businesses during the winter months.
