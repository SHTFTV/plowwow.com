# Langley Strata Snow Removal Cost and Contract Checklist

The cost of strata snow removal in Langley should reflect the property, not a generic unit count. Two 60-home complexes can require completely different service: one may have a short entrance and a few sidewalks, while another has internal drive aisles, visitor stalls, stairs, a parkade ramp and long shaded walkways.

## A Realistic Langley Strata Price Range

As a starting ballpark, many Langley strata properties fall between **$3,000 and $8,000 for seasonal service**. That is not a fixed price or a promise that every site fits the range. A small property with only walkways and one ramp can cost less than a complex with parking stalls, internal roads and repeated ice-control needs. Large or difficult properties require a separate site-specific quote.

The range changes according to the actual work:

*   Total sidewalk and walkway length
*   Parking stalls and drive aisles included in the scope
*   Parkade ramps, slopes and tight turning areas
*   Stairs, landings, mail areas and accessible routes
*   Snow-storage space and whether piles may need relocation
*   Salting versus other ice-melt products
*   Service trigger and the number of return checks required
*   Parked vehicles, gates and time restrictions
*   Equipment that can safely fit the property

A quote based only on the number of homes misses most of these cost drivers.

## Separate Machine Work From Hand Work

Machine clearing and hand clearing should be measured independently. A skid steer or plow can open a drive aisle quickly, but it cannot complete stairs, door approaches, narrow paths or the edges of a ramp. On many Langley townhouse sites, the hand-work portion determines the labour requirement more than the main roadway does.

Ask the contractor to mark each surface on a site plan:

1.  Plowable vehicle areas
2.  Small-equipment areas
3.  Hand-shovelled walks and stairs
4.  Ice-control zones
5.  Snow-storage locations
6.  Surfaces excluded from the contract

This makes proposals easier to compare. It also prevents a disagreement during the first storm over whether a visitor bay, side entrance or interior walkway was included.

## Decide What “Salting Included” Actually Means

The contract should state which areas receive salt or ice melt, what product is expected and whether the material is included in the price. “Salting included” is too vague when one proposal covers only the parkade ramp and another covers every walkway and stair.

The site may also require different treatment zones. Concrete entrances, asphalt drive aisles, finished pavers, landscaping edges and pet routes do not always receive the same product or application. Identifying those surfaces before winter produces a more accurate budget and a clearer work order.

For residential customers on an active Langley route, booked snow-clearing visits start at **$125 per visit when five visits are reserved**. On-call service starts at **$150 per visit** when capacity is available. A salting-only visit is generally priced at 50% of the snow-service rate plus the ice-control product. Driveway length, lot size, hills, stairs and additional walkways can change those starting prices.

## Set the Service Trigger Before the Snow Arrives

A useful contract says when service begins. It should not leave the trigger as “when necessary.” The trigger can be based on accumulation, forecast conditions, observed ice or a client authorization process, but everybody needs to understand it before an event.

The agreement should answer:

*   Is dispatch automatic or requested by the property manager?
*   Which properties receive route priority?
*   Does the first pass open only priority routes or complete every surface?
*   What happens during continuous snowfall?
*   Are overnight refreeze checks part of the scope?
*   How are extra visits approved?

Booked and seasonal properties receive priority over new on-call requests during a storm. That is why a strata should reserve its route position before the forecast changes.

## Account for Parking and Resident Access

Willoughby and Walnut Grove strata properties often have narrow internal streets, visitor parking and vehicles close to the edge of the service area. A contractor cannot clear beneath parked cars, and pushing around them can leave compacted strips that later freeze.

The pre-storm plan should identify where residents move vehicles, how much notice they receive and which areas remain accessible while equipment is working. If visitor stalls are part of the contract, list them. If individual driveways are the owner's responsibility, say so. Clear boundaries help the strata communicate one consistent message.

## Plan for Langley's Different Property Types

Langley City and Langley Township contain very different sites. The quote should change with the setting rather than swapping only the community name.

*   **Willoughby:** dense townhouse and mixed-use properties can require compact equipment, visitor-parking coordination and detailed pedestrian routes.
*   **Walnut Grove:** family-oriented strata communities may combine internal roads, driveway aprons, mail areas and longer sidewalks.
*   **Brookswood and Murrayville:** residential properties can have longer approaches, more landscaping and additional walkways.
*   **Fort Langley:** smaller commercial frontages and residential access may need careful snow storage and hand clearing.
*   **Aldergrove:** route distance and larger properties make advance booking especially important.

These are planning considerations, not automatic assumptions about every address. The final scope should come from the actual site.

## What a Complete Proposal Should Show

Before approving a seasonal contract, confirm that the proposal states:

*   Property address and contract dates
*   Included sidewalks, stairs, ramps, stalls and drive areas
*   Service trigger and dispatch method
*   Priority areas for the first pass
*   Hand work and machine work
*   Salting or ice-melt areas and material pricing
*   Return checks for refreeze or continuing snowfall
*   Approved snow-storage locations
*   Conditions for snow relocation
*   Access instructions and parking restrictions
*   How visits and site issues are reported
*   Price, taxes and any clearly defined extras

If two bids use different scopes, compare the work line by line before comparing the total price.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does seasonal strata snow removal cost in Langley?

Many Langley strata properties fall within a $3,000 to $8,000 seasonal ballpark, but the actual quote depends on sidewalks, parking, ramps, hand work, ice-control scope, access and property size. Large or complex sites are quoted separately.

### Why can two similar-sized strata properties have different prices?

The number of homes does not show the service area. One site may have a simple loop road, while another has long sidewalks, stairs, visitor stalls, a parkade ramp and limited snow storage.

### Is salt included in a Langley strata contract?

Only when the proposal says it is. The contract should identify the treatment areas, product, trigger and whether material is included or billed separately.
