## Microclimate: Wind, Salt Spray, and Cliff-Edge Refreeze

The Point Grey microclimate is dominated by three forces. First, elevation — the cliff-top portion of the neighborhood sits 60+ metres above sea level, with temperatures consistently 2–3°C cooler than False Creek during an arctic outflow event. Second, wind — outflow winds funnel between Bowen Island and Point Grey, hitting NW Marine Drive head-on with sustained 40–60 km/h gusts and 80+ km/h peaks during storm events. Third, marine salt aerosol — the same west wind carries fine sea-salt aerosol up the cliff face, which combines with overnight refreeze to produce a particularly slippery surface texture on stone walkways and exposed-aggregate driveways.

The combined effect: Point Grey driveways and walkways refreeze faster, drift more aggressively, and require a more frequent reapplication of de-icer than any other Vancouver neighborhood. A single clearing-and-salt event is rarely enough for a Point Grey property during a multi-day snow window. Our seasonal contracts include unlimited reapplication runs at no additional cost, which is the only economically sane way to service this neighborhood properly.

## Cliff-Edge Estate Properties

The NW Marine Drive and Belmont Avenue estate stock includes some of the most architecturally significant modernist and contemporary homes in Western Canada. Many feature glass-curtain entries, polished concrete entry decks, basalt or limestone walkways, and bronze or weathering-steel landscape elements. Sodium chloride splash damages every one of those materials. Calcium chloride brine is particularly destructive to weathering steel and to the polished-concrete finishes common on entry decks.

PlowWow's cliff-edge estate chemistry uses:

* Pre-storm traction sand on stone and concrete entry decks
* Calcium-magnesium-acetate (CMA) on driveway aprons and parking pads
* Potassium-formate granules at entries, stairs, and around glass facades
* Magnesium-chloride brine only on the asphalt street apron, never on landscape-adjacent surfaces
* Hand-clearing with insulated grain shovels around any bronze, weathering-steel, or specimen-stone feature

Every Point Grey cliff-edge property gets a pre-season GPS walk-through with the property owner or landscape architect to map material zones and confirm chemistry boundaries. Material-zone maps are reviewed annually because landscape projects regularly add new specimen elements.

## Bylaws and Liability Exposure on the Cliff Edge

Like all Vancouver properties, Point Grey owners must clear sidewalk frontage by 10:00 AM the day after a snowfall (Street and Traffic By-law 2849, Section 76).
