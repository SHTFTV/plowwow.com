## East Cambie's Snow Profile — Why This Corner of Richmond Is Different

East Cambie isn't a standard Richmond residential grid. Its land use and geography produce a distinct set of winter risks:

- **Fraser delta wind exposure.** Sitting between the Fraser and the north arm, East Cambie catches outflow winds. Apparent temperatures drop 4–6 °C below the Richmond weather station reading.
- **Pre-dawn truck traffic.** Cambie Road and Sweden Way run 24/7 distribution operations. Reefer trailers, container drayage and cross-dock ops all move before 6 a.m.
- **Vast surface lots.** Distribution and warehouse sites have some of the largest surface lots in Richmond — clearing them means route planning, not just showing up with a plow.
- **Townhome strata density.** Odlin Crescent, Odlin Road and the residential blocks off No. 4 Road hold dozens of townhome complexes with narrow shared mews that municipal plows never touch.
- **Loading-dock aprons.** The concrete apron between a warehouse door and the drive lane is the highest-injury surface on any distribution site. It also freezes first.
- **SkyTrain pedestrian tributaries.** East Cambie residents walk to Aberdeen and Bridgeport stations on the same sidewalks that bylaw enforcement patrols.

## Distribution & Strata Liability — Occupiers Liability Act and Richmond Bylaw Duty

East Cambie property owners carry two layers of winter exposure.

Under the *Occupiers Liability Act* (BC), an occupier owes a duty to take that care that in all the circumstances of the case is reasonable to see that a person on the premises will be reasonably safe. In distribution and strata case law, "reasonable" has meant a documented pre-open clearing protocol.

Under Richmond Bylaw 8271 and successor consolidations, property owners are responsible for clearing public sidewalks adjacent to their property — typically by 10 a.m. the day after a snow event. Missed sidewalks on Cambie Road and Bridgeport Road are among the most-ticketed frontages in north Richmond.

What this means for an East Cambie landlord or property manager:

- **The 6 a.m. threshold isn't negotiable for distribution.** A contract that promises "morning" service without a written pre-open time doesn't work for a warehouse tenant.
- **Photo documentation isn't optional.** Distribution slip claims and vehicle-damage claims both subpoena the service log.
- **Loading aprons need explicit scope.** Any contract that clears "drive lanes" without naming dock aprons is under-scoped for East Cambie.

1. **Pre-open guarantee.** Seasonal customers are cleared first. Per-event customers wait — often until 10 a.m. instead of 6. For distribution, that's a shift lost.
